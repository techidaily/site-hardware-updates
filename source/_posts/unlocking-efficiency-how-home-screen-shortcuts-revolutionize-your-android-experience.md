##  What Are Home Screen Shortcuts?

 There are three things you can add to the Android home screen---app icons, widgets, and shortcuts. App icons simply launch the app, but [shortcuts can launch directly to a specific section in the app](https://hardware-help.techidaily.com/quick-install-download-your-pioneer-dj-ddjsx2-controller-software/). They're super cool.

 Here are a few examples of some popular apps that have shortcuts. [YouTube](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU206064/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.youtube) allows you to jump straight into a search, the "Subscriptions" tab, or the "Explore" tab.  The pop-up menus shown in the screenshots above are accessed by long-pressing the app icons, but that's only one way to use shortcuts. You can also long-press the shortcut from the pop-up menu and add it directly to the home screen.

 Not all Android apps have shortcuts, but you'll find that [many of them do](https://tech-haven.techidaily.com/resolve-high-cpu-consumption-issues-in-windows-10-a-comprehensive-guide/). ##  How to Add Home Screen Shortcuts on Android

 As briefly mentioned above, home screen shortcuts are all about long-pressing. First, place the app on your home screen and touch and hold the icon.

![Tap and hold the icon.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/2022-05-04_13-41-54.jpg) 

 A pop-up menu will appear with the available shortcuts. You can simply tap a shortcut from here to use it.

![Tap the shortcut.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/2022-05-04_13-43-16.jpg)
