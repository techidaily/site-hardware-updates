##  Why "Clone" an App?

 The most common reason for cloning or duplicating Android apps is using multiple accounts. These days, more apps support signing in with [multiple accounts](https://screen-mirroring-recording.techidaily.com/updated-in-2024-ultimate-roundup-no-fee-videocalling-plus-live-desktop-share/), but there are still plenty of holdouts. Snapchat and WhatsApp are two popular examples.

 When you clone an app, you're creating an identical copy that can be used independently. That means you can sign in with one account on one version and a different account on another. Even if the app does support multiple accounts, you may find it easier to switch between them this way.

 You could also clone an app to create a separate version for a child or anyone else that uses your Android device. The cloning app we'll be using also allows you to change the name of the app if that's something you're interested in doing.

##  How to Duplicate Android Apps

 We'll be using an Android app called "App Cloner" to accomplish this. The app is not available in the Google Play Store, but it can be easily [downloaded from the developer's website](https://appcloner.app/) and [sideloaded](https://facebook-video-content.techidaily.com/updated-unlock-premium-quality-streaming-on-the-worlds-largest-network/) on your device.

 Once installed, open the App Cloner and select the app you would like to duplicate.

![Select the app to clone.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2021-09-23_11-41-08.png) 

 There are a number of things you can do to differentiate the cloned app from the original. First, give the app a different name.

![Change the name of the app.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2021-09-23_11-42-32.png)

 Next, you can tweak the look of the icon, either by changing the color or by rotating it. App Cloner has a bunch of other options that you should explore, but for the purposes of this guide, we'll stop here.

![Customize the app icon.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2021-09-23_11-43-54.png) 

 After you've made your customizations, tap the clone icon at the top.

![Tap the clone icon.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2021-09-23_11-46-51.png)

 Depending on the app you're cloning, you may see a message about functionality issues. Not all apps will work correctly after being cloned. Tap "Continue."

![Tap "Continue."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2021-09-23_11-48-56-1.png) 

 While the app is being cloned, you may also see more warnings. Let the cloning process finish.

![Continue with cloning.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2021-09-23_11-49-09-1.png) 

 Once the cloning is finished, you can select "Install App." You will also need to give App Cloner permission to sideload apps first.

![Tap "Install."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2021-09-23_11-50-49-1.png) 

 The Android APK installer will appear with the new app name and icon you created. Tap "Install" to finish.

![Select "Install App."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2021-09-23_11-51-39.png) 

 Now you can open the newly cloned app and (hopefully) use it like normal. Again, keep in mind that this will not work with every app.

![Launch the cloned app.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2021-09-23_11-52-05-1.png)
