 There are plenty of rear USB ports located on the rear IO plate of the Asus Prime X670-P WiFi, 2 x USB 2.0 Type-A, 4 x USB 3.2 Gen 1 Type-A, 3 x USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-A, and 1 x USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 Type-C.
