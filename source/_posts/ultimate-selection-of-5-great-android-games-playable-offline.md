## 2 [BADLAND](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU2000270/https://play.google.com/store/search?q=badland&c=apps) 

![badland](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/badland.jpg) 

 BADLAND is a dark side-scrolling adventure game that challenges you to survive in a forest full of traps, obstacles, and enemies. You control a fluffy creature and use physics-based mechanics to fly, roll, and bounce through the levels. You can also play with up to four friends in local multiplayer mode or create your own levels with the level editor. BADLAND is free with in-app purchases.

## 3 [Dead Cells](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU2000270/https://play.google.com/store/search?q=badland&c=apps) 

![dead cells](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/dead-cells.jpg) 

 Dead Cells is a Metroid-like hybrid game that combines fast-paced combat, exploration, and progression. You play as a failed alchemic experiment that can possess dead bodies, and you can use them to fight your way through the levels. You can collect weapons, skills, and mutations that enhance your abilities and adapt to different situations. You can also unlock new paths, secrets, and bosses as you explore the game. Dead Cells costs $8.99 to download, plus there are additional in-app purchases that can help you advance.

You have to use logic, timing, and reflexes to overcome deadly hazards such as traps, monsters, and machines. The game is $3.99, but there are no ads or in-app purchases after that.

## 5 [Monument Valley 2](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU2000270/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ustwo.monumentvalley2) 

![monument valley 2](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/monument.jpg) 

 Monument Valley 2 is the charming sequel to the original puzzle game. It follows the journey of a mother and her child as they explore a world of isometric architecture. You have to manipulate the environment, rotate platforms, and create paths to guide them through the levels. You also have to discover hidden secrets and navigate through optical illusions. Monument Valley 2 is a game that will delight you with its colorful art style, melodic music, and emotional story. It costs $3.99 to download, but there are no additional ads or in-app purchases.

## 6 [Plague Inc.](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU2000270/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.miniclip.plagueinc) 

![plague inc](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/plague-inc.jpg) 

 Plague Inc. is an old school strategic simulation game that lets you create and evolve a deadly pathogen that can wipe out the planet. You can choose from different types of plagues, such as bacteria, virus, fungus, parasite, and more. You can also modify your pathogen’s traits, transmission modes, symptoms, and abilities to adapt to various scenarios. It’s a game that feels a little different in a post-pandemic world. Plague Inc. is free to download and contains ads and in-app purchases.

## 7 [Plants vs. Zombies](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU2000270/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ea.game.pvzfree%5Frow), [Plants vs. Zombies 2](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU2000270/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ea.game.pvz2%5Fna) 

![plants vs zombies](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/pvz.jpg) 

 These two long-time favorite tower defense games are both still regularly updated. The game pits you against hordes of zombies that want to eat your brains. You have to use different kinds of plants, such as peashooters, sunflowers, wall-nuts, and more, to defend your house and garden. You can also collect coins, gems, and power-ups to upgrade your plants and unlock new features. Different types of zombies, such as coneheads, bucketheads, and footballers, appear as the game progresses. Both games are free with ads and in-app purchases.

## 8 [Stardew Valley](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU2000270/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.chucklefish.stardewvalley) 

![stardew valley](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/stardew.jpg) 

 Stardew Valley is a relaxing farming simulator that lets you escape the hustle and bustle of the city and start a new life in the countryside. You can create your own character, customize your farm, grow crops, raise animals, fish, mine, craft, cook, and more. You can also interact with the townsfolk, make friends, fall in love, get married, and even have children. Stardew Valley costs $4.99, but it’s a game that you can really immerse yourself into.

## 9 [Super Meat Boy Forever](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU2000270/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thunderful.smbf) 

![super meat boy forever](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/super-meat-boy-forever.jpg) 

 Super Meat Boy Forever is a sequel to the popular platformer game, and it takes place a few years after the original. In this game, you play as Meat Boy or Bandage Girl, who are on a mission to rescue their daughter Nugget from the evil Dr. Fetus. The game features over 5,000 levels that are randomly generated based on your skill level, so you can play the game over and over without seeing the same level twice. The game is very challenging and requires quick reflexes and precise timing to survive. It’s only $0.99 and there are no ads or in-app purchases.

## 10 [Two Dots](https://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/3607085/type/dlg/sid/UUhtgUeUpU2000270/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.weplaydots.twodotsandroid) 

![Two Dots.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/two-dots.jpg) 

 Two Dots is a simple puzzle game that challenges you to connect dots of the same color and clear them from the board. There are different strategies and techniques you can use to create combos, squares, bombs, and more. The art style is cute, and you get to travel through different worlds as you advance through the levels. Two Dots is free with ads and in-app purchases.
