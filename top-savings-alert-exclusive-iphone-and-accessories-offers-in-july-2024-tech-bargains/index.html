How did we choose these Walmart deals?

Walmart is one of the top retailers in the US. You can expect the company to provide year-round bargains, alongside seasonal offers and sales on days such as Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

Alongside the likes of Amazon, Best Buy, and Target, Walmart historically launches great deals on laptops, smartwatches, headphones, IoT home gadgets, and more. We chose our favorite deals based on:

  • Price: While looking for the best deals, we focused on products with an attractive discount. Additionally, we used price trackers to ensure that you are getting a great deal, removing any items that have been purposely price gouged in order to look like a deal.
  • Availability: ZDNET has included deals with good general availability, and we have avoided deals when there are only a handful of products still in stock. That said, stock levels may have changed since the time of publication.
  • Variety: We wanted to include a range of tech products and accessories, whether you’re looking for a new iPad or a modern kitchen appliance. We also ensured our picks matched a variety of budgets.
  • Customer reviews: We took a look at customer reviews and ratings to recommend the best deals and devices.

Does Walmart offer a program similar to Amazon Prime?

In 2020, Walmart began offering Walmart+ . With plans starting at $12.95/month, you can access free deliveries, mobile scanning, fuel discounts, Paramount+, exclusive access to sales and discounts, and more.

Why did Walmart change its name?

Walmart was once known as Wal-Mart Stores. According to Walmart executives, the company changed the name to Walmart in 2017, “to better reflect our company’s path to win the future of retail […] and a bit about returning to the company’s roots.” When the first store opened in 1962, the original name was Walmart, although the organization was incorporated as Wal-Mart.
